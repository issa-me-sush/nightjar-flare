Who this is for

The people who currently pick up the phone.

When someone wants to move real size in crypto today, they do not use a DEX. They message an OTC desk, take a private quote, and settle bilaterally. That entire industry exists for one reason: public books leak intentions. This is what a desk sells, with atomic on-chain settlement and no counterparty who learns your position.

How it works

Sealed in your browser. Matched in an enclave. Settled on-chain.

  1. 01

    Deposit

    Fund a balance, decoupled in time from any order. If you funded the exact size of each trade, the transfer itself would leak it.

  2. 02

    Seal

    Your order is encrypted to the enclave’s public key before it leaves the browser. The venue stores ciphertext and one public number — how many orders are in the batch.

  3. 03

    Clear

    The enclave opens the book, finds the single price that matches the most volume, and signs the result. Everyone in the batch trades at that one price.

  4. 04

    Settle

    The contract checks the signature, re-reads the FTSO feed to bound the price itself, and requires both assets to net to zero before it moves anything.

The enclave is not something new to trust. It never holds your funds — it returns signed balance deltas, and the contract rejects them unless both assets net to exactly zero and the clearing price sits inside a band around Flare's own FTSO feed, re-read on-chain at settlement. A valid enclave signature is necessary, and deliberately not sufficient. The enclave proposes; the chain disposes.
